Privacy

This site collects nothing about you. No cookies, no analytics, no forms, no third-party content.

Hosting

The site runs on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub records access data (IP address, time, page requested, browser) to deliver the site and defend against abuse. I never see those logs. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub is a US company, and the transfer rests on the safeguards set out in their privacy statement.

Email

If you write to me, I keep your message for as long as it takes to answer and to keep a record of it. It goes no further.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and you may object to processing. Write to contact@klausstrassner.com. You may also complain to the Austrian supervisory authority, the Datenschutzbehörde.
