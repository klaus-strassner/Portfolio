Privacy

This site collects nothing about you. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no forms and no embedded third-party content. The typeface is served from this domain, so loading a page makes no request to any other server.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Like any web server, it records access data in server logs — including your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and your browser's user agent — in order to deliver the site and to defend against abuse. I have no access to these logs and receive no reports from them. GitHub's own statement is at docs.github.com.

Legal basis

That processing rests on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: a legitimate interest in delivering this website securely and reliably. Hosting in the United States is covered by GitHub's data transfer safeguards described in the statement above.

Contacting me

If you write to the email address on this site, I hold your message and address for as long as it takes to answer you and to keep a record of the correspondence. That data goes no further.

Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 21 GDPR you may request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing or portability, and you may object to processing. Write to contact@klausstrassner.com.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Austria that is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.
